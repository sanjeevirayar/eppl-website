Predictive Motor Health & Operating-State Analyzer

Condition Monitoring Motor Analysis Predictive Maintenance

A multi-parameter motor condition-monitoring system developed to measure electrical, vibration, thermal and rotational behaviour, identify motor operating states and detect developing deviations from established operating patterns.

← Back to Projects

THE CHALLENGE

Understanding motor condition before failure.

Electric motors can continue running even while electrical, mechanical or thermal conditions are gradually changing.

A single current reading or temperature measurement provides only part of the picture. Motor loading, vibration, temperature and rotational speed can all change for different reasons and must be interpreted within the actual operating state of the machine.

This project addresses that problem by combining multiple sensor inputs, identifying the motor's operating state and tracking how its characteristic behaviour develops over time.

THE CONCEPT

From motor signals to condition insight.

The system measures motor behaviour using several independent sensing channels rather than relying on one measurement alone.

Current measurements represent electrical loading, vibration captures mechanical behaviour, temperature indicates thermal condition and rotational sensing establishes motor speed and operating state.

These signals are processed together to create a baseline for normal operating conditions. New measurements are then compared against those patterns so gradual changes can be detected and presented as condition indicators.

Condition-Monitoring Principle

The system does not attempt to predict an exact failure date. Electrical, vibration, thermal and rotational measurements are evaluated within the motor's operating state and compared with established behaviour so persistent deviations can be identified for maintenance investigation.

02

Motor Operating-State Model

Sensor measurements are interpreted according to the actual motor state so that normal startup behaviour is not treated the same way as steady-state operation.

STATE OPERATING CONDITION SYSTEM INTERPRETATION
S0 Idle Rotational speed and phase current remain near zero while motor temperature gradually approaches ambient condition.
S1 Starting High startup current, changing rotational speed and transient vibration are identified as the normal acceleration period.
S2 Running Current, vibration, temperature and speed are compared with the established steady-state operating baseline.
S3 Developing Deviation One or more parameters show a persistent change from the established operating pattern and are flagged for investigation.

04

Measurement & Safety Philosophy

The analyzer operates as a monitoring system around the motor while conventional starter, overload and circuit-protection devices remain responsible for machine protection.

01

Isolated Current Sensing

LEM HLSR current transducers provide galvanically isolated current measurement between the motor circuit and monitoring electronics.

02

Sensor Placement

The ADXL356 vibration sensor is mounted securely to the motor body so measurement changes represent machine vibration rather than loose sensor movement.

03

State-Based Comparison

Startup measurements are evaluated independently from steady running conditions to avoid false condition indications.

04

Non-Contact Speed Sensing

The IFS252 detects a rotating metallic target without mechanical contact with the motor shaft.

05

Condition Indication

A detected deviation is treated as an indication for investigation rather than an automatic diagnosis of a specific mechanical fault.

06

Existing Protection Remains

Motor overload protection, short-circuit protection and emergency machine-safety systems operate independently from the condition-monitoring platform.

DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

From raw motor signals to condition insight.

The prototype was developed progressively from sensor integration and signal acquisition through operating-state recognition, baseline generation, trend analysis and dashboard visualization.

01

Sensor Integration

Installed current, vibration, temperature and rotational sensors around the motor test arrangement.

02

Data Acquisition

Configured the STM32 acquisition system for synchronized measurement and processing of the different sensor channels.

03

State Recognition

Developed logic to distinguish idle, startup and steady-running conditions using current and rotational behaviour.

04

Baseline Development

Recorded normal operating measurements and established characteristic values for current, vibration, temperature and speed.

05

Trend Analysis

Implemented comparison and trend logic to identify persistent changes from the established motor baseline.

06

Validation & Refinement

Tested the system across different motor states and simulated condition changes, then refined thresholds and processing logic.

01

Monitor electrical, vibration and thermal behaviour of motors driving water or process pumps.

02

Fans & Blowers

Track motor condition in ventilation, cooling and air-handling equipment.

03

Conveyor Systems

Monitor motor loading, rotational behaviour and developing vibration changes in material-handling equipment.

04

Compressors

Observe motor behaviour and condition trends around compressor systems and auxiliary equipment.

05

Production Equipment

Provide condition information for motors operating continuously within industrial production systems.

06

Facility Equipment

Monitor motors associated with HVAC, water systems and other building infrastructure.
