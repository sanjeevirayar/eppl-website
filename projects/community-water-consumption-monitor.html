PROJECT 06 ENGINEERED PROTOTYPE

Community Water Consumption Monitoring & Awareness System

Smart Utilities Water Monitoring IoT

A community water-monitoring system developed to measure water consumption independently for each household, maintain historical usage records, calculate total community consumption and automatically provide every family with a weekly water-usage report.

← Back to Projects

THE CHALLENGE

Making water consumption visible.

Water is consumed continuously across residential communities, but individual households often have limited visibility into how much water they actually use over a day or week.

Without regular consumption information, it can be difficult for families to understand whether their usage is increasing, decreasing or remaining consistent over time.

At community level, the same issue makes it difficult to communicate the scale of collective water use. This project addresses both problems by measuring consumption at each household while also calculating the combined consumption of the entire community.

THE CONCEPT

From water flow to community awareness.

Each household is assigned an independent water monitoring node installed on its incoming water line. The node converts water-flow pulses into accumulated consumption and associates the reading with that specific household.

Household measurements are transmitted through the community network to a centralized monitoring platform, where daily and weekly consumption histories are maintained.

At the end of every week, the system calculates each household's consumption, compares it with the same household's previous week and calculates the total amount of water consumed by the entire community.

Each family receives only its own detailed consumption information together with the shared community total, allowing personal usage to remain private while making collective water consumption visible.

ENGINEERING BUILD

Hardware behind the community monitoring system.

The prototype combines household-level flow sensing, embedded pulse acquisition, long-range wireless communication and centralized data processing to create a distributed community water-monitoring network.

FUNCTION HARDWARE ENGINEERING PURPOSE
Household Water Measurement DFRobot SEN0217 Water Flow Sensor Measures prototype household water consumption.
Household Controller ESP32-DevKitC V4 Counts flow pulses, calculates consumed volume and associates readings with a household ID.
Wireless Communication ESP32 Wi-Fi Transfers household readings to the central monitoring system.
Community Network Wi-Fi Router Connects multiple household monitoring nodes to the server.
Central Processing Raspberry Pi 4 Model B Collects household records, calculates weekly totals and generates consumption reports.

Software Stack: Python · InfluxDB · Grafana · Scheduled Reporting

02

Household Consumption-State Model

Consumption is evaluated against each household's own historical behaviour and configured awareness thresholds rather than publicly ranking households against each other.

STATE CONSUMPTION CONDITION SYSTEM INTERPRETATION
S0 Normal Household consumption remains within its established operating pattern without unusual continuous flow.
S1 Elevated Daily or weekly consumption has increased noticeably compared with the household's recent history.
S2 High Consumption Usage exceeds a configured household awareness threshold and is highlighted within the weekly report.
S3 Possible Continuous Flow Water flow continues for an unusually long period and is flagged for the household to investigate.

04

Measurement, Privacy & Reliability Philosophy

The system is designed around transparent water-use awareness while keeping detailed household consumption separate from community-wide information.

01

Independent Household Records

Every monitoring node is assigned a unique household identity so consumption records remain separated within the database.

02

Private Usage Reporting

Each family receives detailed information only about its own household consumption. Other households' individual readings are not included.

03

Community Total Only

Shared awareness information contains the combined community consumption rather than identifying highest or lowest consuming households.

04

Local Pulse Accumulation

Water consumption is accumulated at the household node so short communication interruptions do not immediately discard flow information.

05

Possible Leak Indication

Extended continuous flow is presented as a possible abnormal condition requiring investigation rather than automatically declaring that a leak exists.

06

Awareness, Not Billing

The prototype is intended for consumption awareness and monitoring. Production billing applications would require appropriately certified metering hardware and installation.

DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

From household flow to community awareness.

The prototype was developed progressively from water-flow measurement and household identification through wireless communication, centralized storage, consumption analysis and automated weekly reporting.

01

Flow Measurement

Integrated the SEN0217 flow sensor and configured pulse counting to convert water movement into accumulated consumption.

02

Household Monitoring Nodes

Configured ESP32-based monitoring nodes with unique household identifiers and local consumption accumulation.

03

Community Communication

Integrated LoRaWAN communication between household monitoring nodes and the centralized community gateway.

04

Data Platform

Developed the Raspberry Pi-based platform for storing timestamped household measurements and maintaining consumption history.

05

Weekly Reporting

Implemented automated calculations for household totals, previous-week comparison, community totals and personalized reports.

06

Testing & Refinement

Tested varying flow conditions, multiple household nodes, communication interruption, weekly aggregation and continuous-flow detection scenarios.
