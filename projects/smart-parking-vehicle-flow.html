PROJECT 05 ENGINEERED PROTOTYPE

Smart Parking & Vehicle Flow Management

Smart Infrastructure Vehicle Access Parking Automation

An intelligent parking-management system developed to detect parking-space occupancy, identify approaching vehicles, manage entry and exit access and maintain real-time visibility of available parking capacity.

← Back to Projects

THE CHALLENGE

Managing more than empty spaces.

Parking congestion is not created only by a shortage of spaces. Vehicles entering without knowing current availability, queues forming at access points and inefficient movement within the parking area can all reduce the effectiveness of the available capacity.

A basic parking sensor can indicate whether one space is occupied, but that information becomes significantly more useful when it is connected to the entry gate, vehicle-identification system and overall parking count.

This project combines occupancy detection, vehicle identification, access control and parking-capacity tracking into one coordinated system that manages both individual spaces and vehicle movement.

THE CONCEPT

From parking detection to vehicle-flow management.

Each monitored parking bay is equipped with an ultrasonic sensor that detects whether a vehicle is present. These individual states are combined to maintain the total number of available spaces.

At the entrance, a vehicle-detection sensor identifies an arriving vehicle while the UHF RFID reader obtains its access credential. The system verifies both vehicle authorization and current parking capacity before an entry command is issued.

Entry and exit events are combined with individual parking-space measurements so that the system maintains an up-to-date view of parking usage, access activity and available capacity.

ENGINEERING BUILD

The prototype combines industrial ultrasonic sensing, vehicle detection, long-range RFID identification, PLC-based access logic and edge data processing to coordinate parking occupancy and vehicle movement.

System Principle

The parking controller manages access logic and parking-state decisions, while the monitoring platform records bay availability, entry / exit activity and overall parking utilization for operator visibility.

02

Parking & Access State Model

Parking availability and access conditions are converted into operating states so entry decisions are based on both vehicle identity and actual facility capacity.

STATE PARKING CONDITION SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
S0 Available Parking spaces are available and the system accepts authorized vehicle entry requests.
S1 Entry Requested A vehicle has been detected at the entrance and its RFID credential and facility capacity are evaluated.
S2 Access Granted Vehicle authorization and parking capacity are valid, allowing the barrier opening request to be issued.
S3 Full / Restricted Normal entry is prevented when calculated capacity reaches zero or when the access request is not valid.

04

Access & Safety Philosophy

Parking automation manages access decisions and occupancy information while physical barrier safety remains within the dedicated barrier-control system.

01

Barrier Control Separation

The PLC sends access commands through an isolated relay interface rather than directly powering or controlling the barrier motor.

02

Barrier Safety Remains Local

Motion control, obstacle handling and other barrier-specific functions remain with the FAAC barrier control system.

03

Sensor Validation

Parking occupancy is updated only after sensor readings remain within the configured occupied or available range.

04

Count Reconciliation

Total parking capacity is derived from individual bay states rather than relying only on entry-minus-exit counting.

05

Operator Override

The HMI provides an operator-supervised method for handling visitor access, sensor faults and exceptional vehicle movement.

06

Uncertain State Handling

Conflicting occupancy or access information is displayed for operator review rather than automatically assuming additional capacity.

DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

From space detection to vehicle-flow control.

The prototype was developed progressively from individual parking-space sensing through access detection, PLC logic, RFID integration, barrier control and centralized monitoring.

01

Parking Layout & Instrumentation

Defined monitored parking bays, entry and exit points and installed the occupancy and vehicle-detection sensors.

02

Occupancy Detection

Configured ultrasonic distance measurements and converted them into stable available and occupied parking states.

03

Vehicle Identification

Integrated the long-range UHF RFID reader and linked registered vehicle identifiers with entry-access decisions.

04

Access-Control Logic

Developed PLC sequencing for vehicle detection, capacity verification and isolated barrier command generation.

05

Dashboard & Operator Interface

Developed parking views showing individual bay status, total availability, barrier state and vehicle-access events.

06

Testing & Refinement

Tested vehicle entry, occupied-bay changes, full-capacity conditions, RFID access, exit events and inconsistent sensor scenarios.
