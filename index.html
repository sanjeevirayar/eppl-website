ENGINEERING WORK

Ideas become working systems.

Explore our engineering concepts and development work across embedded systems, IoT, automation, monitoring and networking.

ELECTRICAL · AUTOMATION

Smart Electrical Panel

An intelligent electrical panel developed to monitor power demand, prioritize connected loads and automatically manage selected circuits when available capacity becomes constrained.

PLC · Power Monitoring · Load Control

CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE · MONITORING

Data Center Infrastructure Monitor

An integrated monitoring system developed to bring electrical, cooling, battery and environmental infrastructure information into one operational view.

Modbus · Raspberry Pi · Grafana

INDUSTRIAL · AUTOMATION

Equipment & Production Monitor

An industrial automation system developed to monitor machine condition, production activity and operating state while coordinating PLC-based equipment control.

Siemens PLC · VFD · Industrial Sensors

CONDITION MONITORING · ANALYTICS

Predictive Motor Health Analyzer

A multi-parameter motor monitoring system developed to observe electrical, vibration, thermal and rotational behaviour and identify developing operating deviations.

STM32 · Sensors · Raspberry Pi

SMART INFRASTRUCTURE · AUTOMATION

Smart Parking & Vehicle Flow

A parking-management system developed to monitor space occupancy, identify vehicles, manage entry and exit access and maintain real-time parking capacity.

PLC · RFID · IoT

SMART UTILITIES · IOT

Community Water Consumption Monitor

A community water-monitoring system developed to measure household consumption, track usage history and provide families with weekly individual and community-wide consumption awareness.

ESP32 · LoRaWAN · Raspberry Pi

OUR PROCESS

02

03

04

05

Implement
